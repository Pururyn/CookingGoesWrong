# 🍳 Cooking Goes Wrong

A chaotic survival game built in **Unreal Engine (Blueprints)**.

A mischievous imp has cursed a chef, trapping her in an endless nightmare. In this nightmare, her own kitchen utensils have come to life — and they're out for revenge. To break the curse, the player must collect stickers scattered across the map and return them to the imp, all while surviving waves of angry cookware.

## 🎮 Gameplay

- Survive in a restaurant overrun by rogue kitchen utensils
- Collect **stickers** that randomly spawn across the map
- Return the stickers to the **imp** to escape the nightmare
- Weapons periodically spawn to help you **fight back**
- The utensils will relentlessly chase you — don't get caught!
